1) You could run a PEX extraction on the final chip layout and run a spice simulation on the result. Probably most accurate but also slowest.
2) You could use the SDF data from OpenROAD to run a verilog simulation with timing. I'm not sure what open-source verilog simulator supports that. I think CVC but I never tried.
3) You could use the OpenSTA to query the expected delay for your specific path. Either by specifying a max delay for it in the sdc or just directly query in an interactive shell.
