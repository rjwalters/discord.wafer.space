[2026-11-19 9:12 p.m.] namibj
<https://gf180mcu-pdk.readthedocs.io/en/latest/IPs/IO/gf180mcu_fd_io/features.html> says the foundry IO cells are 75um pitch; so taking the maximum configuration `1x1_max_all` from <https://mith.ro/gf180mcu-project-template/>, which says 200 pads, and going from the aspect ratio of the core area to calculate that it must probably be 42 horizontal pads and 58 vertical pads, which is supported by the pad count of `1x1_max_top`&`1x1_max_lft`, we get that it's `27um` over 31 pads horizontally and `7.8um` over 43 pads vertically while meeting the 4mil pitch limit of that JLC process.

(||Extra spacing between IO cells for JLC pitch minimum is only needed between pads, not outside to a corner, so all but one can be counted as `101.6um` wide and the last one can count with it's natural `75um` width it occupies on the die regardless of bonding considerations.||)

**So, 148 total pads "easily"**; IMO it should be relatively easy to put power pads over the interior circuity once we have proof it works; for CUP-concern-reasons of emergency wire bonding, leaving some of the interior pads at least partially free of underlying circuity so that a wedge bonder could hit there, should _I think_ suffice.
Plan would be to use the 2nd layer of the flex to feed in power and GND and use some vias in probably the central area of the die with deliberate lack of bonding pressure from the press-head to via it through to the layer that's ACF-bonded, and spread to some power pads that form sort of a second row on the inside of the pad ring (for DRC aspect reasons probably not right over the power ring but instead just inwards over the outer region of the core area; IIRC the power ring uses top metal for sheet resistance reasons to spread the supply around the ring).
