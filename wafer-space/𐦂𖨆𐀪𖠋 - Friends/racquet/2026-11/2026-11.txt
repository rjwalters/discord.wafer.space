# Welcome to #🎾-racquet
This channel is for **Racquet**, @Greg's (Greg Davill) multicore RISC-V SoC, and the chips made on wafer.space runs. @Greg looks after this channel.

Racquet is built from many [SERV](<https://github.com/olofk/serv>) cores. SERV is the award-winning bit-serial RISC-V CPU by Olof Kindgren (olofk), the world's smallest RISC-V CPU.

**Chips**
- Run 1: [GD02](<https://wafer.space/ws-run1/projects/GD02.html>) (9 cores, 0.5x1 slot), [GD03](<https://wafer.space/ws-run1/projects/GD03.html>) (23 cores, 1x1 slot) and [GD04](<https://wafer.space/ws-run1/projects/GD04.html>) (6 cores, 1x0.5 slot)
- Run 2: [GD04](<https://wafer.space/ws-run2/projects/GD04.html>) (6 cores, 1x0.5 slot)

**Source**
- [gf180mcu-racquet](<https://github.com/gregdavill/gf180mcu-racquet>)
- [gf180mcu-racquet-0.5x1](<https://github.com/gregdavill/gf180mcu-racquet-0.5x1>)
- [gf180mcu-racquet-1x0.5](<https://github.com/gregdavill/gf180mcu-racquet-1x0.5>)
- [SERV](<https://github.com/olofk/serv>) and its [docs](<https://serv.readthedocs.io/en/latest/>)

If you have one of these chips, please post your bring-up results, boards, firmware and questions here.
