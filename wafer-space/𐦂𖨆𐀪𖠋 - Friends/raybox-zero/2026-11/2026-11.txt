# Welcome to #📺-raybox-zero
**raybox-zero** is @algofoogle (Anton Maurovic)'s hardware ray caster: a tiny GPU that races the beam of a VGA display to draw a Wolf3D-style "3D" world. Anton looks after this channel.

It has flown on Tiny Tapeout several times and now has wafer.space silicon too:
- Run 1: inside both Tiny Tapeout GF 0.2 chips, [TTP2](<https://wafer.space/tinytapeouts/ttgf0p2/ws-run1-ttp2/projects/135.html>) and [TTPG](<https://wafer.space/tinytapeouts/ttgf0p2/ws-run1-ttpg/projects/135.html>)
- Run 2: a die of its own, [AMM1](<https://wafer.space/ws-run2/projects/AMM1.html>) (1x0.5 slot), with SPI control, an optional QSPI texture ROM and Tiny VGA compatible RGB222 output

The design lives in [raybox-zero](<https://github.com/algofoogle/raybox-zero>), the Run 2 chip in [gf180ws2-raybox-zero](<https://github.com/algofoogle/gf180ws2-raybox-zero>), and the bring-up and devkit boards in [gf180ws2rbz](<https://github.com/algofoogle/gf180ws2rbz>).

Anton will post his bring-up board progress here. If you have one of these chips, share your boards, videos and questions too.
